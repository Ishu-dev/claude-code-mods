# Candidate profile for the interview coach
Copy this to the path set in PROFILE (hooks/register.tsx) and fill it in. The coach reads it on every answer and builds its "better examples" from it, so list only facts and numbers you can defend.

- Programme, school, batch, and when placements start
- Two or three strengths you want interviewers to remember
- One delivery habit to work on (for example: speaking too fast)

## Experiences to draw examples from
1. Internship or job, with dates and clients:
   - What you built or changed, with a before/after number
   - What you would not claim (prototype vs deployed)
2. Leadership role:
   - Team size, what you started, measurable outcome
3. Project or competition:
   - Problem, your part, result
